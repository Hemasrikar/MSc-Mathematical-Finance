## MSc Mathematical Finance

This repository contains my personal notes and study material from the MSc Mathematical Finance programme at the University of Warwick.

I found the programme to be very expensive relative to what it offers, so I decided to organise and share my own notes and learning material publicly. I hope this repository can be useful to anyone who is interested in Mathematical Finance, wants to explore what the MSc programme covers at Warwick, or is looking for a way to learn some of the material without committing to the cost of a full MSc.

These are my personal notes and are not official University of Warwick teaching materials.
